# Executive Summary — FY2025
### Nusantara Digital Group · Software & Digital Marketing · Indonesia
*All figures in IDR millions. Simulated data for portfolio purposes.*

---

## The headline
The company grew revenue **+66.3%** across FY2025 (IDR 3,946 mn in Jan → IDR 6,561 mn in Dec) and remained solidly profitable, delivering **IDR 14,391 mn net profit** on **IDR 59,187 mn revenue** — a **24.3% net margin**. The opportunity now is **margin quality**, not top-line growth.

## What's driving results
- **Seasonality:** Two demand peaks — Lebaran (Mar–Apr) and Harbolnas (Nov–Dec) — shape the revenue curve. The year-end peak is strongest.
- **Margin mix:** SaaS Creator Platform has the best **gross margin (69.7%)**; Influencer Marketing the best **net margin (30.8%)**; Ads Management drives **volume** at the lowest margin (20.5%).
- **Cost concentration:** Operational cost is **46.6% of revenue** — the single biggest lever for profit.
- **Budget gap:** ~IDR 14,690 mn underspent (75.6% utilization) — buffers are too large and may be starving growth bets.

## Three actions for next quarter
1. **Reallocate investment** toward SaaS + Influencer Marketing (highest-margin lines).
2. **Reduce operational cost** in Ads Management and rationalize SaaS licences (target 3–5 pts).
3. **Tighten budgeting** — zero-based by service line, ±5% variance tolerance, redeploy structural underspend.

## Outlook
Linear forecast projects Q1 2026 revenue of **~IDR 18,690 mn** (Jan 6,057 · Feb 6,230 · Mar 6,403), with upside if seasonal Lebaran demand is captured.
